Privacy by design

Privacy & Data Practices

We design every app with security, transparency, and control for every user. These principles outline how we handle information in Entellix products and client solutions.

What we collect

We only collect the minimum information needed to deliver and improve the app experience, such as account details, usage analytics, and device diagnostics. Sensitive data (health, finance, biometrics) is gathered only when explicitly required for features and with consent.

How we use data

Data is used to operate core features, maintain reliability, and enhance usability. We do not sell personal data. Access to information is restricted to authorized team members who need it to support the product.

Storage & retention

Information is stored using modern encryption in transit and at rest. Logs and analytics are retained only as long as necessary for troubleshooting and reporting, after which they are aggregated or deleted.

Third-party services

When we rely on trusted vendors (cloud, analytics, payments), they must follow equivalent security standards and data protection regulations. We avoid sharing personal data unless it is required to provide a specific service.

User controls

Users can request access, correction, export, or deletion of their data through in-app settings or by contacting us. We honor platform-level privacy choices such as notification and location permissions.

Security posture

We apply secure coding practices, routine penetration testing, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring. Incidents are triaged quickly with transparent communication to affected users or clients.

Questions or requests

Contact the Entellix privacy team.

Reach out for data inquiries, security disclosures, or to request app-specific privacy details.
